    • setSubdomain(
          name: string,
          options: { enabled: boolean; previews?: boolean },
      ): Promise<SubdomainState>

      Turns the workers.dev hostname on or off.

      previews is separate and worth setting deliberately: a managed preview wants exactly one URL, so it enables the subdomain and turns per-version preview URLs off. Preview URLs also carry no logs at all, which is the other reason not to rely on them.
